# 05. Stakeholders

| Stakeholder | Interest | Key Question They Care About |
|---|---|---|
| CEO / Founder | Overall growth sustainability | "Is our growth driven by new or repeat customers?" |
| Head of Marketing | Acquisition ROI | "Which cohorts justify our acquisition spend?" |
| Head of CRM / Retention | Repeat purchase programs | "Where should we focus retention campaigns?" |
| Category Managers | Category-level performance | "Which categories drive repeat purchases?" |
| Finance | Revenue forecasting | "How much revenue comes from repeat vs. new customers?" |
