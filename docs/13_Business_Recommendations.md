# 13. Business Recommendations

## Recommendation 1 - Launch a first-60-day re-engagement trigger
**Action:** Automated email/discount campaign targeting all first-time buyers between day 15 and day 45 post-purchase (before the natural month-1/month-2 window closes).
**Rationale:** Insight 1 and 3 - retention is both extremely low overall and concentrated (relatively) in months 1-2; this is the highest-leverage window before customers disengage entirely.
**Expected Impact:** Even a conservative lift from 0.33% to 1.5-2% month-1 retention would multiply the repeat-buyer base 4-6x at a fraction of new-customer acquisition cost.

## Recommendation 2 - Investigate root cause before scaling acquisition further
**Action:** Run qualitative/product research (delivery experience, seller quality, marketplace UX) to understand *why* repeat purchase is this low — this level of one-and-done behavior is unusual even for marketplaces and likely points to a specific friction point (e.g., long delivery times, seller inconsistency, one-off marketplace usage patterns).
**Rationale:** Insight 2 - retention did not improve organically even as acquisition volume grew 8x, meaning the fix is unlikely to be "more scale" and is more likely a specific, addressable friction point.
**Expected Impact:** Identifying and fixing even one major friction point could shift the retention curve meaningfully rather than incrementally.

## Recommendation 3 - Track retention as a core KPI going forward, not just revenue/GMV
**Action:** Add month-1 and month-3 retention rate to the standard executive dashboard, cohort-by-cohort, so any future intervention's impact is visible immediately.
**Rationale:** Insight 2 - without cohort-level tracking, the business would not have surfaced this problem in the first place, since topline order volume was growing the whole time.
**Expected Impact:** Enables fast feedback loops on Recommendation 1 and 2 - a successful intervention should be visible as a clear step-change in month-1 retention within 1-2 cohort cycles.
