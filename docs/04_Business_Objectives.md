# 04. Business Objectives

## Primary Objective
Quantify customer retention behavior by monthly acquisition cohort to identify where and why customers stop returning.

## Secondary Objectives
- Identify which acquisition cohorts (by month) retain best/worst
- Determine typical time-to-repeat-purchase
- Segment retention patterns by relevant dimensions (category, state, order value) if data supports it
- Provide actionable recommendations to improve repeat purchase rate
